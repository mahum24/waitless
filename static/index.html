Same treatment. Months sooner.

In England you have a legal right to choose which hospital you're referred to, and waits for the same specialty differ by months. WaitLess reads your referral, compares hospitals using the latest NHS England waiting-time data, checks inspection ratings and recent news, then writes the letter to send your GP.
